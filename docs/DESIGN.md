## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a hygienic, crisp, and deeply reliable identity tailored for CV. Tiga Putra Teknik—a technical AC service provider and spare parts distributor based in Pangkep. It takes direct inspiration from contemporary high-standard home maintenance interfaces, uniting the crystalline clarity of treated air with rigorous, honest engineering.

The brand balances two fundamental pillars:
1. **Pristine Hygiene & Cool Comfort:** Clean surfaces, arctic-bright contrast, and subtle cool cerulean accents that instantly evoke fresh airflow, clean filters, and immaculate workspaces.
2. **Generational Usability & Accessibility:** Designed with deliberate care for a wide spectrum of users—ranging from younger digital natives booking on the go to mature homeowners, senior building managers, and local workshop operators (aged 50+). This demands zero ambiguous abstractions, high contrast ratios, an uncompromising minimum font size of 16px for body content, and minimum touch targets of 44px across all touchpoints.

### Aesthetic Movement: High-Contrast Modern Utility
The visual aesthetic merges **Modern Corporate Utility** with **Tactile Cleanliness**. Surfaces rely on brilliant white raised panels sitting over a neutral cooling mist background (`#F5F7FA`), crisply defined by subtle structural borders (`#E3E8EF`), soft ambient shadows, and pill-shaped touch nodes. Visual noise is eliminated; typography is bold, reassuring, and immediate.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an immediate atmosphere of refreshing coolness, mechanical precision, and rigorous visual order.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#1E63D6`)**: Active blue. Applied to key interactive touchpoints, primary order buttons, prominent focus highlights, active tab states, and major service selection tiles.
- **Secondary (`#14407F`)**: Deep industrial blue. Grounds headings, major hero titles, technical callouts, and foundational structural section headers.
- **Tertiary (`#20B7C9`)**: Crisp air cyan. Serves as a targeted micro-accent for cooling icons, active diagnostics, air filtration indicators, and transit tracking badges (`dikirim`).
- **Neutral (`#F5F7FA`)**: Ice-mist canvas. The universal page backing that provides depth behind clean white cards without the harsh glare of plain white paper.

### Functional & Semantic Mapping
- **`surface-tint` (`#E8F1FD`)**: Soft sky-blue wash utilized for informational callout banners, gentle card states, and category overlines.
- **`surface-dark` (`#0F1F44`)**: Deep midnight navy utilized for structural anchoring (global footer, high-contrast modal backdrops, and technical badges).
- **`border-subtle` (`#E3E8EF`)**: Structural hairline dividers ensuring card boundaries remain perceptible for users with decreased contrast sensitivity.
- **`text-primary` (`#1F2937`) & `text-secondary` (`#5B6675`)**: Strictly controlled contrast ratios exceeding WCAG AA standards (4.5:1 for body copy).
- **Status Indicators**:
  - `menunggu` (neutral gray): `#5B6675` text on `#E3E8EF` background.
  - `dijadwalkan` (blue): `#1E63D6` on `#E8F1FD`.
  - `dikerjakan` (amber): `#B45309` on `#FEF3C7` (derived from `#F2A93B`).
  - `dikirim` (cyan): `#0E7490` on `#E0F2FE` (derived from `#20B7C9`).
  - `selesai` / `dikonfirmasi` (green): `#15803D` on `#DCFCE7` (derived from `#1E9E6A`).
  - `dibatalkan` (red): `#B91C1C` on `#FEE2E2` (derived from `#D64545`).

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to ensure high geometric legibility, wide apertures, and clean counters. This font remains distinctly readable on both high-DPI displays and low-spec mobile devices.

### Accessibility Rules
- **Minimum Body Size:** Standard interactive body copy, inputs, table descriptions, and metadata never drop below `16px` (`body-md`). This protects readability for users aged 50+ without requiring browser zoom adjustments.
- **Auxiliary Labels Only:** Font sizes below 16px (`label-md` at 14px and `label-sm` at 13px) are strictly restricted to secondary metadata, status pill tags, and uppercase tracking tags accompanied by icons.
- **Headline Rhythm:** Hero headlines use strong font weights (700 and 800) paired with tighter line-height proportions to prevent loose wraps when technical terminology or long Indonesian titles appear.

## Layout & Spacing

The system is constructed on an intentional **8px rhythmic spatial grid** (`0.5rem` increments) to maintain balance between open commercial whitespace and high-density technical service portals.

### Layout Architecture
- **Fluid Desktop Grid:** 12-column grid constrained to a maximum content container width of `1280px`, with a default gutter of `1.5rem` (24px) and outer margin of `2rem` (32px).
- **Floating Main Canvas (Hero Panel):** Key interactive sections, such as the booking calculator or hero action center, sit on an elevated, pure-white floating card structure inset against the `#F5F7FA` canvas with `2.5rem` internal padding.
- **Mobile Adaptations (< 768px):** The grid collapses into a single-column linear stack. Outer margins contract to `1rem` (16px). Multi-card service grids collapse from 4 columns into a 2-column or horizontal scroll layout.
- **Physical Touch Target Rule:** All buttons, touchable cards, step controls, and input triggers must maintain a strict minimum target height and width of `44px` (recommended `48px` to `56px` for primary calls to action).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through a pairing of **tactile surface layers** and **ambient cool-tinted shadows**, evoking a clean, polished, "shiny surface" environment.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Ground Tier (`#F5F7FA`):** The cooling ambient background canvas.
2. **Elevated Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority structural panels and cards featuring crisp `1px solid #E3E8EF` borders and an ambient diffuse shadow (`0 10px 30px -5px rgba(20, 64, 127, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(20, 64, 127, 0.03)`). The shadow is tinted with `#14407F` (deep blue) rather than dead carbon black, sustaining the fresh, hygienic tone.
3. **Floating Interactive Tier:** Dropdowns, dialogs, estimation summaries, and the floating hero booking card (`0 20px 45px -10px rgba(15, 31, 68, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -4px rgba(15, 31, 68, 0.04)`).
4. **Anchor Tier (`#0F1F44`):** High-density footer and primary navigation bars that frame the canvas.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Rounded (`roundedness: 2`)** base shape profile, scaled upward to larger radii for structural containers to provide an approachable, modern feel.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Structural Cards & Modals (`rounded-2xl` / 16px):** Primary content containers, service option cards, parts catalogue items, and pricing estimate boxes.
- **Form Controls & Inputs (`rounded-lg` / 10px - 12px):** Booking fields, dropdown triggers, and text areas.
- **Pill Shape (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Primary hero buttons ("Pesan Layanan Sekarang"), process step badges (01, 02, 03), contact buttons, and order status chips (`dikerjakan`, `selesai`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill CTA):** Full pill (`rounded-full`), background `#1E63D6`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold `16px` font, vertical padding `14px`, horizontal padding `28px`, minimum height `48px`. On hover, color transitions to `#14407F` with a soft blue glow (`0 8px 20px rgba(30, 99, 214, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary Action:** White background, `1px solid #E3E8EF`, text `#14407F`, `rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`. Hover shifts background to `#E8F1FD` and border to `#1E63D6`.
- **Destructive Action:** `#D64545` background with white text, or transparent background with `#D64545` outline for cancellation workflows.

### Service & Catalog Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white panel, `16px` border radius, `1px solid #E3E8EF`.
- **Card Icon:** Inset inside a `48px × 48px` rounded square with `#E8F1FD` background, using single-color stroke line icons colored `#1E63D6` or `#20B7C9`.
- **Selected/Active State:** Cards can toggle into an active state featuring a solid `#1E63D6` background with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text and white stroke iconography, directly confirming user selection.
- **Pricing & Spare Part Cards:** Clear price tags presented in `18px` bold `#1E63D6`, paired with explicit stock availability badges.

### Status Chips (Pills)
- Pill shape (`rounded-full`), height `28px` to `32px`, padding `4px 12px`.
- Consists of an `8px` solid circular dot paired with semibold `13px` or `14px` text in Bahasa Indonesia.
- Must never rely on color alone; always pair the background tint with a descriptive text label.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Metrics:** Minimum height `50px` to guarantee effortless finger placement for older users.
- **Visuals:** White surface, `1px solid #E3E8EF`, `12px` border radius, `16px` typography (`#1F2937`).
- **Focus State:** `2px solid #1E63D6` with a soft outer ring `rgba(30, 99, 214, 0.15)`. Labels remain permanently docked above the field at `14px` bold to prevent disorientation during entry.

### Cost Estimation Calculator Panel
- A distinct, transparent cost calculation module.
- Elevated on white background with a light blue header strip (`#E8F1FD`).
- Granular line-item breakdown (service fee, refrigerant/freon type, spare part unit costs) with prominent, easily legible summary totals displayed in `24px` `#14407F`.
